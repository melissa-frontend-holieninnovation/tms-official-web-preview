About us

One connected delivery operation.

SenditGo brings route planning, dispatch, live tracking and proof of delivery together for local and last-mile fleets.

Built around the delivery day.

Build runs, assign orders and review the day in Order Management. Drivers follow their run and record delivery details, while dispatchers keep track of progress in one place.

Meet SAM, your planning engine.

SAM stands for Smart Adaptive Model. It brings your delivery requirements and available vehicles together to help you build a workable route plan.

Delivery locations and time windows, your warehouse and vehicle capacity give the plan its starting point. You review the proposed plan in Order Management and stay in control of what goes out.
