SENDITGO WEBSITE · INTERIM POLICY

Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information when you visit the website, register interest in SenditGo or make an enquiry.



1. Who we are

2019 Group Pty Ltd (ABN 16 633 010 723) is the contact for privacy enquiries about the public SenditGo website. Our contact details are in section 9.

This policy covers the public website and expressions of interest. Separate information is available for the driver app and delivery services. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles, and the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 where it applies.

2. What we collect
When	What we collect
You enquire or register interest	Your name, email address, enquiry type and the details you submit. Depending on your enquiry, these can include your business name, fleet size, delivery operations, software systems and preferred dates. Providing a phone number is optional.
You browse	Our hosting and font providers receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver the pages you request.

You can browse without identifying yourself. We need your contact details to respond to an enquiry. Please leave out sensitive information, individual delivery records, tracking numbers and personal information about other people.

3. How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, assess interest in SenditGo and follow up about the early access, trial or launch information you requested. We also use information to operate, secure and improve the website and meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information or use it for behavioural advertising.

4. Cookies and third-party services

The website stores your theme and cookie preferences in your browser. The marketing pages do not currently use optional analytics cookies. You can change your preference at any time.



Google Fonts receives technical information to deliver typefaces. The delivery demonstrations are recordings served by the website. Watching them does not send a location or tracking number to a map provider.

5. Who we share it with and where it goes

Authorised personnel and service providers may handle personal information to host the website, process enquiries and send email. Where the enquiry form is available, it sends the information you enter to our Google-hosted form processor.

Service providers or support staff may process information overseas. We handle overseas disclosures in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and may also disclose information where required or authorised by law.

6. Marketing

Enquiring about SenditGo allows us to respond to that request. We send other news or offers about SenditGo or 2019 Group services only if you separately agree to receive them.

You can unsubscribe using the instructions in a marketing message or contact us using the details in section 9. We process applicable marketing unsubscribe requests free of charge within five working days.

7. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. We retain enquiry and expression of interest records for as long as needed to respond, manage the relationship and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete your information.

8. Your rights and complaints

Use the details in section 9 to request access, ask us to correct your information or make a privacy complaint. We aim to respond to complaints within 30 days. We handle access and correction requests within applicable statutory time limits. In New Zealand, we usually respond to access requests within 20 working days, subject to any lawful extension.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, in Australia you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner on 1300 363 992. In New Zealand, contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner on 0800 803 909.

If a data breach meets applicable notification requirements, we will notify affected individuals and the relevant regulators as required by law.
