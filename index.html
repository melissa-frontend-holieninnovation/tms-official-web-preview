Smart Adaptive Model

Meet SAM. The best offsider your dispatch desk ever had.

SAM takes your drops, delivery windows and fleet, and turns them into a route plan that fits: the right stops in the right order on the right vehicle. Then SAM hands it back to you. Your fleet, your call.

  1. Load your runs.

    Drop addresses, delivery windows, your depot and what each vehicle can carry. That’s all SAM needs to get going.

  2. SAM does the heavy lifting.

    SAM works every stop around your distance, drive-time and capacity limits. It hands back a run sheet for each vehicle with the stop order, kilometres and time on the road.

  3. You sign it off.

    Check the plan in Order Management, adjust what you need, then dispatch. SAM does the legwork. You run the show.
