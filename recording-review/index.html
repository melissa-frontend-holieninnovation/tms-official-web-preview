SenditGoOfficial website · Recording brief

One order, two phone screens.

Use the same test order on both phones. The prototype shows SG-R09-1042 at Riverside Pharmacy. Before submitting proof, match the DEV order reference in the driver app and the consignee’s signed-in account on the official website. The driver submits proof of delivery (POD). The consignee then signs in on the official website, opens the same order and sees Delivered.

Recording 1 · Driver phone

Submit proof of delivery

Open the demo stop in the driver app. Show the order reference, capture the proof requested by the app, and tap submit. For the staged POD photo, group the three demo parcels beside a plain doorway. Shoot upright from one step back so all three parcels and the threshold fit in frame. Keep faces, house numbers and shipping labels out of shot. Hold on Delivery recorded. This is the Driver V2 confirmation that the proof was received. If the proof is still uploading or queued offline, wait for acceptance before recording the customer phone.

Recording 2 · Consignee phone

See the same order delivered

After the driver sees Delivery recorded., use a mobile browser to sign in to the SenditGo official website as the consignee. Begin the clip on My deliveries after sign-in. Open the same DEV order and hold on Delivery tracking. Frame the matching order reference and the Delivered status together.

For the edit  Keep both recordings in portrait. Cut from the driver’s confirmation to the consignee opening the order in the mobile browser, with Delivered visible. Use the same demo order reference throughout.
