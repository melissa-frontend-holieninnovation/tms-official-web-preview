SenditGoOfficial website · Recording brief

One order, two phone screens.

Record one demo delivery from both sides. The driver submits proof of delivery (POD). The customer then opens the shareable tracking link for that order and sees it marked Delivered.

Recording 1 · Driver phone

Submit proof of delivery

Open the demo stop in the driver app. Show the order reference, capture the proof requested by the app, and tap submit. Hold on the confirmation so it can be read.

Recording 2 · Customer phone

See the same order delivered

After submission, open that order’s shareable tracking link on a second phone. Keep the matching order reference and the Delivered status on screen long enough to read.

For the edit  Keep both recordings in portrait. Cut from the driver’s confirmation to the customer’s Delivered screen. Use the same demo order reference throughout.
